# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-16

First public release.

- Desktop stickers you can move freely, with snapping to neighbouring stickers and screen edges.
- Checklists and plain notes, each with an editable title.
- A completion date (`dd.mm.yyyy`) appears under each checked item.
- Templates: Eisenhower matrix (4 stickers) and Brain dump (12 stickers).
- Pin a sticker above all windows.
- Privacy blur that reveals an item when the mouse rests on it.
- Close without deleting, plus a trash you can restore from.
- Collapse a sticker to its title.
- 4 default colors, 10 pastels and custom colors.
- Autosave, plus autostart through Task Scheduler.
- Embedded Onest font. Uses TT Fors instead when it is installed.
